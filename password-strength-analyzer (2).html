How scoring works

1. Character pool. Using lowercase, uppercase, digits, and symbols together makes each position harder to guess than sticking to one type.

2. Length compounds it. Entropy grows with length, not just variety — bits ≈ length × log₂(pool size). A long passphrase can beat a short "complex" password.

3. Patterns cost you. Dictionary words, keyboard runs ("qwerty"), and repeats ("aaa") are the first things crackers try, so they're penalized even if the character mix looks strong.
